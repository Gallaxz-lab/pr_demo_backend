Finance module — Phase 3

Concept preview: how settlement reconciliation and invoice OCR will work, with sample data

1. Marketplace settlement reconciliation

Shopee, Lazada and other channels pay out in batches, not per order — a settlement report lists many transactions at once, each with fees already deducted. The system needs to match each payout line back to the exact order it belongs to, automatically.

1 Platform exports payout batch
2 System reads reference + net amount
3 Matches against your order records
4 Confirmed → auto-reconciled. Unclear → flagged for review

2. Invoice OCR → draft accounting entry

A supplier invoice photo gets read automatically and turned into a draft record — someone still reviews and approves it before it posts to the books, but the manual re-typing is gone.
